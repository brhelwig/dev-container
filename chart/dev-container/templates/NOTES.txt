Get a shell in your dev container:

  kubectl exec -it --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} statefulset/{{ include "dev-container.fullname" . }} -- zsh

Or join the persistent zellij session the pod starts on its own:

  kubectl exec -it --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} statefulset/{{ include "dev-container.fullname" . }} -- zellij attach dev
