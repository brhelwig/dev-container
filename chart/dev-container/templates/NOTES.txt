securityContext.privileged: true by default). If the Deployment never
reaches Ready, check that:
  - you're on GKE Standard, not Autopilot (Autopilot rejects privileged pods)
  - the target namespace doesn't enforce a "restricted" or "baseline"
    Pod Security Standard
